Mention privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Mention is made by an independent developer in the United States. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email mentionfitnessapp@gmail.com.

The short version

Your records stay on your device. Mention's developer doesn't run servers for the app and never receives your food log, photos, chats or API keys. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Data leaves your device only when you use a feature that needs an outside service, and this policy lists each one.

What Mention keeps on your device

Mention stores your profile, goals, dietary preferences, meals, recipes, water, weight entries, workouts, supplement schedules with taken and skipped records, and your chat conversations. These records stay only on your device. They're excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so deleting the app erases them. To keep a copy, make a backup file in Data & export.

AI provider keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. Mention doesn't keep food photos you choose for an estimate; the original stays wherever it was, such as your photo library. Progress photos are kept inside the app with their dates and notes. Mention resizes them and removes location details, never sends them to an AI provider, and includes them in a backup file only when you turn that option on.

AI features you choose to use

Mention sends nothing to an AI provider until you add your own key for that provider and turn on Allow sending food data for it in Settings, under AI providers. Turning that switch off or removing the key stops all sharing with that provider.

When you ask for an estimate or chat, your device sends your request straight to the provider you picked, using your own account with that provider. Depending on the feature, the request contains the food description or photo you submitted, recipe text from a page you chose to import, and for chat, recent messages, today's food totals, your targets and your dietary preferences. Weekly reviews send a summary of your food log. Apple Health data and progress photos are never sent.

The provider handles your request under its own terms and your account settings, which can differ by plan and region. Some free plans, such as the Gemini API free tier, may use what you send to improve their services and allow human review. Don't submit anything you don't want that provider to receive. Provider policies: Google Gemini API, OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI.

Food databases and recipe websites

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, Mention sends the search words or barcode to USDA FoodData Central or Open Food Facts. It never sends your food log. When you import a recipe, Mention downloads the public web page you chose. Like any website, these services see ordinary network details such as your IP address.

Siri, reminders and email

Siri and Shortcuts requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and Mention saves only what you confirm. Supplement reminders are local notifications created on your device; their text can appear on your Lock Screen, depending on your notification settings. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply, and delete the conversation within 12 months after it ends.

Not in this version

This version of Mention has no accounts, no cloud sync, no shared recipes, no purchases and no Apple Health connection. If a future version adds any of these, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

How your data is used and shared

Your data is used only to provide the features you ask for. Mention doesn't sell or rent data, doesn't share it for advertising and doesn't build profiles. The developer doesn't share your data with anyone; the services above receive data only when you use the feature that needs them, and only directly from your device.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or a similar region: the developer doesn't receive or store your app records, so most data protection obligations are met by keeping the data on your device under your control. Requests you send to an AI provider are covered by your agreement with that provider. Emails you send us are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering them.

Keeping, exporting and deleting your data

Records stay on your device until you delete them. You can:

  • edit or delete any single record;
  • save everything to a backup file in Data & export;
  • erase all of Mention's data in Data & export;
  • remove an AI key or turn off sharing for a provider in Settings, under AI providers;
  • change camera, photo and notification permissions in your device's Settings app.

Erasing or deleting the app doesn't remove backup files you saved elsewhere or copies a provider already received. Contact that provider to ask about its copies. To ask about anything else, including access or deletion requests, email mentionfitnessapp@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Mention is meant for people aged 18 and older. Its calorie targets are designed for adults. The developer doesn't knowingly collect data from children and doesn't receive app records from anyone. If you believe a child is using Mention, email us and we'll help.

Security

Mention connects to outside services only over HTTPS and stores keys in the Keychain. No method is perfectly secure. To report a security issue, email mentionfitnessapp@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, this page shows the new effective date. For important changes, we'll also say so in the app update's release notes before they take effect.
