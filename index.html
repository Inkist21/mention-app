Mention support

Mention is a calorie and macro tracker you can talk to. Describe a meal in your own words, take a photo or scan a barcode, check the estimate, then log it.

Contact: mentionfitnessapp@gmail.com.

Privacy policyTerms of use

Turn on AI estimates

Mention uses your own key from an AI provider. In Mention, open Settings, then AI providers. Choose a provider, paste your key, and turn on Allow sending food data. Gemini offers a free tier. Food search, barcode scanning, water, weight and workout logging work without a key.

Log with Siri

Say "Log Mention". Siri asks whether you want to log a meal, water, a workout or your weight, then checks with you before saving. Say "Today's calories in Mention" to hear your totals.

Back up or move your records

Your records stay on your device and aren't included in iCloud backups. To keep a copy or move to a new device, open Settings, then Data & export, and make a backup file. Restore it from the same screen.

Delete your data

Delete single entries by swiping them away, or erase everything in Settings, then Data & export. To stop sharing with an AI provider, turn off its switch or remove its key in Settings, then AI providers.

Something's wrong

Email mentionfitnessapp@gmail.com with what happened, your device and iOS version, and a screenshot if you can. To report an AI reply, touch and hold it in Chat, then choose Report reply.
